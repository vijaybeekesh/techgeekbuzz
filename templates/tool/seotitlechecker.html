    SEO Title Best Practices

    • Keep titles concise and descriptive - aim for around 50-60 characters as a starting guideline.
    • Put your main keyword where it naturally fits - usually earlier in the title, without forcing it.
    • Make every page title unique - duplicate titles make it harder for search engines and users to tell pages apart.
    • Avoid unnecessary keyword repetition - one natural mention is usually enough.
    • Write titles for users first - a title that reads naturally will always outperform one stuffed with keywords.
    • Check both character count and pixel width - wide letters like "W" and "M" take up more space than narrow ones like "i" and "l".
    • Preview how your title may appear in search results - and adjust if it looks like it will be cut off.
    • Remember length limits are guidelines, not rules - search engines can rewrite or truncate titles regardless of length.
