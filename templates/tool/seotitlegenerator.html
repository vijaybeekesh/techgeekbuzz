SEO Title Tips

  • Keep it under 60 characters — Google typically truncates longer titles in search results.
  • Front-load your keyword — placing it near the start helps both readers and search engines.
  • Use numbers and power words — "7 Tips" and words like "Proven" or "Essential" boost click-through rate.
  • Avoid keyword stuffing — one natural mention of your keyword is enough.
  • Make every title unique — duplicate titles across pages confuse both users and search engines.
  • Match search intent — a "Buy" page needs a different title style than a "Learn" page.
