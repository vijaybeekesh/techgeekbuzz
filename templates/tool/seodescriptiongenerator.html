Meta Description Tips

  • Aim for 140–160 characters - Google typically truncates longer descriptions in search results.
  • Include your primary keyword naturally - ideally near the start, so it's clear the page matches the search.
  • Avoid keyword stuffing - one natural mention of your keyword is enough.
  • Lead with the benefit - tell searchers what they'll get, not just what the page is.
  • Use action-oriented language - words like "Discover," "Get," or "Learn" encourage clicks.
  • Make every description unique - duplicate descriptions across pages confuse both users and search engines.
  • Avoid generic filler - phrases like "Welcome to our website" waste valuable space.
