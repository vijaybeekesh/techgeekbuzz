    What is GST?

    GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a single, unified indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India, replacing multiple older taxes like VAT, service tax, and excise duty.
    GST is charged at different rates depending on the category of goods or services — commonly 3%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28% — and is split between the Central and State governments for transactions within a state, or collected as a single Integrated GST for transactions between states.

    How GST is Calculated

    Adding GST

    GST Amount = Original Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100. Total Amount = Original Amount + GST Amount.

    Removing GST

    Original Amount = Inclusive Amount × 100 ÷ (100 + GST Rate). GST Amount = Inclusive Amount − Original Amount.

    CGST, SGST & IGST

    For sales within the same state, the GST amount is split equally into CGST and SGST. For sales between two different states, the full GST amount is charged as IGST instead.
