What is a Password Generator?

A password generator is a tool that creates a random or customized password for users based on the given guidelines or rules. It aids in the creation of robust and unpredictable passwords that offer a high degree of protection for the specific type of access.
Strong and secure password generators work by incorporating secure technology and built-in randomness to create strong passwords that are difficult to crack. Also, they adjust guidelines to accommodate the varying password requirements of different websites.

What Makes a Strong Password?

Long

Passwords with longer lengths are more secure. The length of a strong password should be at least 10 characters.

Complex

A strong password should be complex. This implies it should include numbers, letters, cases, and symbols to create an unpredictable string of characters that does not resonate with any name or word.

Unique

A strong password should be unique to each account. This reduces the vulnerability in the case of a cyberattack.
